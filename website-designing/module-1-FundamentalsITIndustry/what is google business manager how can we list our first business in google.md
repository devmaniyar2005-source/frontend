# what is google business manager how can we list our first business in google ?
1. Google Business Profile (often referred to as Google Business Manager) is a free tool that allows owners to manage their online presence across Google Search and Maps i.e called Google Business
2. Google Business helps customers find your business, leave reviews, and see essential details like hours and location i.e called Google Business
3. Listing a business is 5 main steps ...

**steps to list a business**
1. Sign in to Google Business
2. Enter Business Name and Category
3. Add Location Details
4. Enter Contact Information
5. Verify the Business

# Sign in to Google Business ...

1. Step where you log into your Google account and navigate to the official business profile creation page i.e called sign in

    **examples**
  
    ```
    Using an existing Gmail account
    ```

# Enter Business Name and Category ...

1. Step where you type the exact name of your business and select its primary industry or service type i.e called enter name

    **examples**

    ```
    Software Development Company
    ```

# Add Location Details ...

1. Step where you provide your physical office address for Google Maps or specify your service areas if you operate online i.e called add location

    **examples**

    ```
    Selecting "No physical location" for digital services
    ```

# Enter Contact Information ...

1. Step where you add your business phone number and website URL so customers can easily reach you i.e called enter contact

    **examples**

    ```
    +91 9876543210
    ```

# Verify the Business ...

1. Step where Google confirms you actually own the business, usually through a phone code, email, or video recording i.e called verify

    **examples**

    ```
    Entering a 6-digit SMS verification code
    ```
