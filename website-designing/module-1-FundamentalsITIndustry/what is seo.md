# What is SEO?

1. SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It is the strategic process of improving a website's structure, content, and authority to increase its visibility on search engine results pages (SERPs) like Google, Bing, or Yahoo.

2. The primary goal of SEO is to drive organic (unpaid and natural) traffic to a website. When a site ranks higher for specific keywords or phrases relevant to its business or content, it is much more likely to attract clicks and visitors.
