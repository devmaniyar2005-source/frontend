# what is www ?

 1. The World Wide Web (WWW), or simply "the Web," is a global information medium that users can access via computers connected to the internet. It consists of billions of interconnected digital pages and resources that are formatted in a way that allows them to be easily viewed and navigated.

 2. Invented by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989, the Web was originally designed as a way for scientists to share information easily.
